## What it does

SpaceBay turns your WordPress site into a community. Members join spaces, post, comment and react in a fast portal that works on desktop and mobile, in light and dark mode.

SpaceBay is **free** on [WordPress.org](https://wordpress.org/plugins/spacebay/). **SpaceBay Pro** is an optional add-on with calls, two-factor login and tools for community managers. Features that need Pro are marked <span class="sb-badge sb-badge--pro">Pro</span>.

<img src="/spacebay/assets/screenshots/portal-admin-light.png" alt="The SpaceBay portal home page with a welcome banner and the community feed" width="1440" height="900" data-lightbox="true" />

## Core capabilities

- Spaces and groups: public, private and secret.
- A feed with images, link previews, comments, likes, saved posts, topics and hashtags.
- Events with going, maybe and can't go answers, and Add to calendar.
- Private messages and group chats.
- Profiles and a member directory.
- Points, levels, badges and a leaderboard.
- Notifications, emails and search.
- Moderation: reports, blocked words, member approval and community moderators.
- Login and signup pages in your design, with Google and Facebook login.
- Calls, two-factor login, a signup form builder, custom profile fields, a badge editor, event limits and member shops with Pro. <span class="sb-badge sb-badge--pro">Pro</span>

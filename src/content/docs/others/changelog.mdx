## 1.1.0

### SpaceBay 1.1.0

- New: the post box is a short bar that opens a focused window, full screen on phones. It has a live character counter, a clear reason when **Post** is off, a collapsed **Add title and topics** section and a **Discard?** check.
- New: set the longest post (500 to 50,000 characters) and comment (200 to 10,000) under **Settings → Posts and uploads**. Lowering a limit never locks old posts: a long post can still be edited shorter.
- New: filters `spacebay_post_max_length`, `spacebay_comment_max_length` and `spacebay_message_max_length`.
- New: short text-only posts can show in larger type. An admin switch and a character limit (140 by default, 40 to 280) control it.
- New: Site Health tells you when your site seems to be behind a proxy or CDN and shows how to fix it with the `spacebay_client_ip` filter.
- Changed: `/community/register` now goes to the sign-up page.
- Security: login limits count every spelling of one account together and also cap failed logins per address.
- Security: the members directory and profile pages show only people who belong to the community, not every site user such as shop customers.
- Security: starting chats, creating groups and adding people to groups is limited per hour. The first email to someone who has not written in a chat has no message text, chat title or sender name.
- Security: only community members can be @mentioned or messaged by other members. Replies in a chat stay open.
- Fixed: long posts are no longer cut short without warning when the limit was raised.

SpaceBay Pro stays at 1.0.0 and works with SpaceBay 1.1.0.

## 1.0.0

### SpaceBay 1.0.0

- Initial release.
- Spaces and groups, feed, comments, likes, saved posts, topics and hashtags.
- Profiles, member directory, notifications and emails, search.
- Private messages and group chats.
- Events with going, maybe and can't go answers, a calendar, Add to calendar and notifications.
- Points, levels, nine ready-made badges and a leaderboard.
- Community login, signup and password reset pages, Google and Facebook login, limits on login attempts.
- Moderation, reports, blocked words, member approval and suspension, community moderators.
- Setup wizard, branding, light and dark mode, privacy export and erasure, REST API, hooks, template overrides and WP-CLI sample content.

### SpaceBay Pro 1.0.0 <span class="sb-badge sb-badge--pro">Pro</span>

- Initial release.
- Voice and video calls in chats.
- Two-factor authentication with an authenticator app, codes by email and backup codes.
- Signup form builder and custom profile fields.
- Badge editor, and badges and points given by hand.
- Event place limits and reminders.
- Member shops.

SpaceBay Pro 1.0.0 needs SpaceBay 1.0.0 or newer.

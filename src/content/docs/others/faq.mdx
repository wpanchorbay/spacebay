---

## Is SpaceBay free?

Yes. SpaceBay is free on WordPress.org. SpaceBay Pro is a separate paid add-on. See [Free and Pro](/spacebay/pro/overview/).

## Do I need the free plugin to use Pro?

Yes. SpaceBay Pro is an add-on. It needs SpaceBay installed and active, and WordPress 6.5 or newer. See [Requirements](/spacebay/getting-started/requirements/).

## Does Pro have a licence key?

No. You install the Pro zip and activate it like any plugin. See [Licence and activation](/spacebay/pro/licence-and-activation/).

## Where do members use the community?

On the community pages, which SpaceBay adds to your site. They work on phones and desktops, in light and dark mode. See [The community portal](/spacebay/user-guide/community-portal/).

## Can I use SpaceBay inside my theme?

Yes. In **Settings → Appearance**, set **Layout** to **Inside your theme**. See [Compatibility](/spacebay/others/compatibility/).

## Can members sell things?

With SpaceBay Pro, members can open a shop and list products that link to their own store. SpaceBay never takes payments. See [Member shops](/spacebay/pro/member-shops/).

## Can people log in with Google or Facebook?

Yes. See [Log in with Google or Facebook](/spacebay/user-guide/social-login/).

## Does SpaceBay work on multisite and in right-to-left languages?

Yes. It was tested on multisite and in Arabic. See [Compatibility](/spacebay/others/compatibility/).

## Can I export or erase a member's data?

Yes, with the WordPress privacy tools. See [Privacy and personal data](/spacebay/user-guide/privacy-and-gdpr/).

## What happens to my data if I delete the plugin?

Nothing, unless **Delete all SpaceBay data when the plugin is deleted** is on. See [Updates and uninstall](/spacebay/others/updates-and-uninstall/).

## How do I add demo content?

Use WP-CLI: `wp spacebay demo`. See [WP-CLI](/spacebay/developers/wp-cli/).

## Where can I get help?

- **SpaceBay (free):** ask in the [SpaceBay support forum on WordPress.org](https://wordpress.org/support/plugin/spacebay/).
- **SpaceBay Pro:** get help at [wpanchorbay.com](https://wpanchorbay.com/spacebay-pro/).
- **Bought on CodeCanyon:** you can also use the Support tab on the CodeCanyon item page.

Please include your WordPress version, PHP version, the SpaceBay (and Pro) version and the steps that cause the issue. The latest documentation is at [docs.wpanchorbay.com/spacebay](https://docs.wpanchorbay.com/spacebay/).

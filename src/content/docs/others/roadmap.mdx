This page shows the direction we plan to take SpaceBay. Everything here is **planned**, not released, and plans change as we learn from the people who use it. Next, Planned and Later show the order we have in mind, without dates. What has actually been released is in the [changelog](/spacebay/others/changelog/).

Items marked <span class="sb-badge sb-badge--pro">Pro</span> are for SpaceBay Pro. Where an item has no mark, we have not yet settled whether it belongs to the free plugin or to Pro. The free plugin stays fully usable on its own.

## Next

- **Community availability.** Open your community, close it for a while with a notice and an optional countdown, or hide it completely. Managers can still get in.
- **Space cover photo.** A banner image for each space, in place of the plain colour band.
- **More feed sorting.** Besides Latest and Recent activity, sort a feed by oldest, most liked, most commented or most engaging.
- **Typing indicator.** See "Sarah is typing…" in one-to-one and group chats.
- **Where your community is active.** A block on the dashboard with likes, comments, posts, event RSVPs and new members for a period you choose.

## Planned

- **Emoji picker.** Add emoji to posts and comments without leaving the page.
- **Polls.** Ask members a question with a few options and let each member vote once.
- **Recurring events.** Weekly, monthly or custom repeats, with changes to one event or to all of them.
- **Event answers and places.** Filter the events you are going to by Going, Maybe or Can't go, see the events you host, and see places left on the events list, not only on the event page. <span class="sb-badge sb-badge--pro">Pro</span> for the places left.
- **Event cover image.** A picture for each event.

## Later

- **Share buttons and links to a single comment.** Share a post or a comment, and open a link straight to that comment.
- **Link previews.** A title, description and image when a post link is pasted elsewhere, for posts anyone can read.
- **Calendar feed.** Subscribe to the events you are going to from your own calendar app.
- **Outbound webhooks.** Send community activity to other tools, signed and retried. <span class="sb-badge sb-badge--pro">Pro</span>

Missing something? Tell us in the [support forum](https://wordpress.org/support/plugin/spacebay/) on WordPress.org.

:::note
SpaceBay Pro has **no licence key** to enter. Install the zip and activate the plugin.
:::

## Getting Pro

Buy SpaceBay Pro on the [SpaceBay pricing page](https://wpanchorbay.com/plugins/spacebay/#pricing) and download **spacebay-pro.zip** from your account. Pro is an add-on: it needs the free SpaceBay plugin installed and active, and WordPress 6.5 or newer. Pro has no licence key.

## Install and activate

1. Install and activate the free SpaceBay plugin first.
2. Download **spacebay-pro.zip** from your wpanchorbay.com account.
3. Go to **Plugins → Add New Plugin → Upload Plugin**, choose the zip, click **Install Now**, then **Activate**.

## Updates

Pro has no licence check and no built-in updater, so WordPress does not offer Pro updates on its own. The plugin header points at wpanchorbay.com/plugins/spacebay, which also keeps WordPress.org from offering updates for it. To update it:

1. Download the new **spacebay-pro.zip** from your wpanchorbay.com account.
2. Go to **Plugins → Add New Plugin → Upload Plugin**, choose the zip and click **Install Now**.
3. Confirm **Replace current with uploaded**.


Your community data is kept during updates. SpaceBay Pro 1.0.0 needs the free plugin at SpaceBay 1.3.0 or newer. Update the free plugin first, then Pro.

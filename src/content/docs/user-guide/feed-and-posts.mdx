Members post in the spaces they belong to. The **Home** feed shows posts from every space the member can read, newest first; **Recent activity** puts posts with new comments first. Each space has its own feed on its **Posts** tab, where pinned posts stay at the top.

## Writing a post

- Click the post box. It opens a window (full screen on a phone) where you write, choose a space and press **Post**. <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> or <kbd>Cmd</kbd> + <kbd>Enter</kbd> also posts.
- A counter appears once you are close to the limit and shows how many characters you are over. When **Post** is off, a short note says why.
- If you close the window with text in it, you are asked **Discard?** (since 1.1.0).
- The **Add title and topics** section is closed at first. Open it to give the post a heading (the title is optional) and to choose topics.
- The image button, next to **Post**, adds up to 10 JPG, PNG, GIF or WebP images. Large photos are resized and their hidden data, such as GPS location, is removed.
- Choose up to three topics.
- Type `@` and a name to mention a member. Only people who belong to the community can be mentioned; any other name stays plain text (since 1.1.0).
- A post with only text, no title, images or link, and no longer than the short-post limit is shown in larger type. Admins can change this under [Posts and uploads](/spacebay/user-guide/settings/general/#posts-and-uploads).

| Type | To get |
| --- | --- |
| `**bold**` | **bold** |
| `*italic*` or `_italic_` | *italic* |
| `` `code` `` | `code` |
| Lines starting with `- ` or `1. ` | A list |
| Lines starting with `> ` | A quote |
| `@name` | A mention linking to the member |
| `#word` | A hashtag |
| A web address | A link |

## Link previews and videos

YouTube and Vimeo links show a video player. Nothing is loaded from YouTube or Vimeo until someone presses play. Other links show a preview card with the page title, description and image when **Show link previews** is on in Settings. To build the card, your site requests the linked page once and stores the result for a day. Turn the setting off if you do not want your site to contact linked websites.

## Who can post

Active members of a space can post. In a space's details, **Who can start posts** can be set to **Only space admins and moderators**, which suits announcement spaces; members can still like and comment. Authors can edit and delete their own posts. Space admins, moderators and WordPress administrators can edit, delete and pin any post in the space.

## Comments, likes and saved posts

Members of a space comment on its posts and reply to comments. Replies nest up to 5 levels deep, with a top-level comment counting as level 1. A reply to a comment on the last level appears next to that comment instead of below it. Developers can change the limit with the `spacebay_comment_max_depth` filter. Members can like posts and comments, and **Save** any post they can read; saved posts appear on the **Saved** page, which only they can see.

<img src="/spacebay/assets/screenshots/comments.png" alt="A post with a comment and nested replies, each reply indented under the comment it answers" width="1440" height="900" data-lightbox="true" />

*Comments and nested replies.*

Authors can edit and delete their own comments. Space admins, moderators and WordPress administrators can edit and delete any comment in the space. What happens to the replies depends on who deletes:

- When a space admin, moderator or WordPress administrator deletes a comment, every reply below it is deleted too.
- When the author deletes their own comment, replies from other members are kept and move up to the deleted comment's parent, or to the top level if it had none. The author's own direct replies are deleted with the comment, and the replies to those move up as well.
- When a member's account is erased through the privacy tools, their comments are deleted the same way, so other members' replies stay.

To slow down spam, a member can publish 5 posts and 20 comments per minute. WordPress administrators are not limited. Developers can change the limits with the `spacebay_rate_limit` filter.

## Topics and hashtags

Create topics such as Questions, Ideas or Events in **SpaceBay → Topics**. Members add up to three topics to a post, and everyone can filter the home feed by topic. Hashtags are created automatically from `#words` in posts; clicking one shows every post with that hashtag.

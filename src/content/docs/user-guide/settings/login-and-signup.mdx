---
title: "Login and signup settings"
description: "Community login pages, letting people sign up, the terms page, and the Pro signup form and two-factor settings."
---

Open **SpaceBay → Settings** and choose the **Login and signup** section in the left column. Select **Save changes** at the top right when you are done.

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Community login pages | Members log in, sign up and reset their password on pages that match your community. On by default. Turn it off to use the WordPress login page. See [Login and signup](/spacebay/user-guide/login-and-signup/). |
| Let people sign up | Opens the signup page. On a new site it follows the WordPress setting *Anyone can register*. |
| Terms page | Address of your terms. When an address is set, the signup form shows an “I agree to the terms” tick box that links to it. |
| Signup form <span class="sb-badge sb-badge--pro">Pro</span> | Which fields are on the signup form, which are required, and their order, with a live preview. |
| Two-factor authentication <span class="sb-badge sb-badge--pro">Pro</span> | **Off** (default), **Optional** or **Required for everyone**. See [Two-factor authentication](/spacebay/pro/two-factor-authentication/). |
| Require it for admins and moderators <span class="sb-badge sb-badge--pro">Pro</span> | With **Optional**: accounts that can manage or moderate the community always need a code. |
| How members get their code <span class="sb-badge sb-badge--pro">Pro</span> | Authenticator app or email (default), authenticator app only, or email only. |

Google and Facebook buttons are turned on in the same section. See [Log in with Google or Facebook](/spacebay/user-guide/social-login/).

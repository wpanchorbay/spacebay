---
title: "Points and badges settings"
description: "Points for each action, level names, the leaderboard and the nine ready-made badges."
---

Open **SpaceBay → Settings** and choose the **Points and badges** section in the left column. Select **Save changes** at the top right when you are done.

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Points, levels and badges | Shows points, levels and badges on profiles and next to names. On by default. See [Points, levels and badges](/spacebay/user-guide/points-badges-and-leaderboard/). |
| Leaderboard | The Leaderboard page with this week, this month and all time. On by default. Hidden profiles are left out. |
| Points for each action | A published post (5), a comment (2), a like from someone else (1, goes to the author), a visit once a day (1), answering “going” to an event (2). 0 to 100 each; 0 turns that action off. |
| Level names | Up to 12 levels, each with a name and the points needed. The first level starts at 0. Default: Newcomer, Member (50), Regular (200), Contributor (500), Veteran (1,000), Legend (2,500). |
| Badges | Switch each of the nine ready-made badges on or off. |
| Badge editor <span class="sb-badge sb-badge--pro">Pro</span> | Up to 40 badges. Add badges, rename them, and choose their colour, icon and rule: given by hand, or earned at a number of posts, comments, likes received, points, daily visits or events. |
